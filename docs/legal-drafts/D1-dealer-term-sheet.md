# Cridora × [Dealer Legal Name] — Term Sheet

**Purpose:** Non-binding summary of the commercial and compliance structure for Cridora's Marketing & Technology Services Agreement with [Dealer Legal Name] ("Dealer"), a licensed Dubai Precious Metals & Stones (DPMS) dealer trading as [Dealer Trading Name], [DMCC / DED / other] license no. [_____].

**Date:** [meeting date] · **Prepared by:** Cridora · **Status:** For discussion, not for signature.

---

### 1. What this is
A Marketing & Technology Services Agreement — **not** a sales partnership, agency, or joint venture. Cridora brings verified buyers and runs the technology/compliance rails. The Dealer sells the gold, holds the money, and fulfills the order. The sale contract is always **customer ↔ Dealer**; Cridora is never a party to it and never takes title to gold or custody of the gold-principal payment.

### 2. Indemnity
Dealer indemnifies Cridora for: authenticity, purity, and weight of all metal supplied; order fulfillment and collection/custody handling; and the Dealer's own trade license, AML/CDD, and DPMSR/goAML compliance. Cridora indemnifies Dealer for platform/technology failures within Cridora's control.

### 3. Fulfillment SLA
Dealer allocates and serializes the customer's specific item (bar/coin, serial number recorded) within **[3] business days** of cleared, name-matched funds. Missed SLA triggers the cancellation/refund terms in the Customer ToS.

### 4. Exclusive spread (required — deal-breaker term)
Dealer's price to Cridora customers must be **at least ~1% better** than Dealer's own walk-in retail price, so a customer's all-in cost (gold + Cridora's processing fee) is never worse than buying direct at the counter. This is funded by marketing spend the Dealer isn't paying for, and is the mechanism that removes the customer's incentive to bypass the platform after their first order.

### 5. Buyback
Dealer guarantees repurchase of platform-originated gold at spot-minus-stated-spread, honored within **48 hours** of customer request. Cridora receives **30–50%** of the Dealer's buyback spread on platform-originated buybacks (exact % to be agreed). No customer funds or gold balance are held by Cridora at any point in this process — Cridora is paid its spread share by the Dealer, separately from the customer transaction.

### 6. Custody (optional free storage)
If Dealer offers free short-term storage instead of immediate collection:
- Title passes to the customer at invoice; Dealer holds the item as **bailee**, not owner. No serial number = no valid order.
- A digital custody receipt is issued automatically (item, serial, customer name, storage end date).
- Dealer's insurance policy must carry a **third-party custody / bailee-goods rider** covering customer-owned stored items; certificate on file with Cridora before storage is offered to any customer.
- **30-day maximum** per item, then a forced event: collect, buyback, or (once available) migrate to insured vault storage.
- Total value in Dealer custody at any time is capped at **AED 300,000–500,000** during the pilot; Cridora's platform blocks new stored orders past this cap.
- Monthly photo/video reconciliation of stored items against platform records, provided to Cridora.

### 7. KYC / compliance responsibility
Dealer is the regulated DPMS and legally owns customer due diligence (CDD): reviews and approves/rejects each customer's KYC submission before they may buy. Cridora is the system of record — it hosts the KYC checklist, approval workflow, audit log, and AED 55,000 cumulative-exposure monitoring/flagging — but does not perform CDD itself. Customer documents are shared with Dealer only through the Cridora dashboard (never WhatsApp/email), consistent with the customer's PDPL consent.

### 8. Non-circumvention
Dealer agrees not to solicit platform-introduced customers to transact outside Cridora, and Cridora agrees not to introduce the Dealer's existing customer relationships to a competing dealer, for the term of this agreement plus [12] months.

### 9. Fees
Cridora charges the customer a processing fee (currently: greater of AED 49 or 0.9%, capped at AED 499, + 5% VAT) as a separate card payment. **The Dealer pays no commission or fee to Cridora on the base transaction** — Cridora's dealer-side revenue is limited to the buyback spread share in §5 and, for future additional dealers, a platform subscription. Gold principal is paid by the customer directly to the Dealer's bank account by bank transfer only; the sender name must match the KYC name before Dealer confirms funds. No cash, no crypto, no card payment on gold principal.

### 10. Term and exit
Initial term: [6] months from signature, auto-renewing unless either party gives [30] days' written notice. Either party may terminate for material breach (e.g. failed fulfillment SLA, indemnity breach, non-circumvention breach) with [X] days to cure.
