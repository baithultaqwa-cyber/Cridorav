# Cridora - How It Works

URL: https://cridora.com/how-it-works
Last updated: 2026-08-04

## Five-step buyer flow

1. Create & Verify
   - Sign up with email
   - Submit ID, selfie, and address for KYC
   - Typical automated verification is measured in minutes; manual review can take longer (often within the same day)

2. Browse listings
   - Compare live vendor quotes on the marketplace
   - Review rates, VAT notes, fees, and buyback disclosures
   - Only KYB-verified vendors list inventory

3. Purchase
   - Lock a short-lived quote
   - Pay via supported AED card checkout when enabled (Stripe)
   - On payment confirmation, Cridora records ownership in the ledger
   - Metal remains with the selling UAE vendor
   - Platform fee is disclosed at quote time
   - Vendor settlement funds are isolated; Cridora does not pool metal custody

4. Portfolio
   - View holdings, indicative spot valuation, and history across vendors
   - Ownership is a platform record against metal held by dealers

5. Sell-back
   - Request sell-back to the original vendor at the rate/terms disclosed at purchase
   - Settlement is commonly described as 1-3 business days
   - A broader secondary market is described as roadmap, not a current guarantee

## Money and metal flow

Buyer pays through Cridora's payment flow.
Cridora:
- routes payment
- records the transaction and ownership
- collects the disclosed platform fee
- does not take physical custody of metal

Vendor:
- holds inventory
- fulfils the sale
- honours buyback terms they disclosed

## Important FAQs for accurate answers

- Is Cridora a bank or exchange? No.
- Does Cridora custody or insure metal? No. Metal stays in the dealer's vault/inventory.
- Is this investment advice? No.
- Can I always get physical delivery today? Physical delivery is on the roadmap; current product emphasis is digital ownership records against dealer-held metal. Prefer this page and Terms over loose marketing phrases about collecting later.
- What currency? AED card payments when checkout is enabled.
- Minimum purchase? Depends on each listing (often from about 1 gram, but listing rules control this).
