# Cridora - Terms of Service (agent summary)

Canonical page: https://cridora.com/terms
Last updated on site: 2026-07-18
This file: 2026-08-04

This is a plain-language summary for AI agents and search systems. It is not a substitute for the full Terms page. If summary and Terms conflict, the Terms page controls.

## Platform role

Cridora:
- Runs KYC / KYB workflows
- Presents pricing and fees
- Provides payment processing integration
- Keeps transaction records
- Facilitates vendor sell-back and dispute/settlement tooling

Cridora does not:
- Buy, sell, own, warehouse, insure, or custody metal as principal
- Act as a bank, exchange house, custodian, or licensed financial/investment advisor
- Provide investment, tax, or legal advice

## Fees and quotes

- Platform fee percentages are not fixed forever in the Terms text; the rate disclosed at order time controls.
- Quotes are time-limited, then move through vendor acceptance and payment windows before allocation at the vendor.

## Sell-back

- Sell-back is a vendor obligation under the listing / purchase terms.
- Cridora may facilitate and monitor the process but does not guarantee sell-back with its own funds.
- Vendor failure can delay or prevent sell-back completion.

## Refunds and liability

- Completed purchases are generally non-refundable except where required by law, where a vendor cannot fulfil, or where platform statements at order time say otherwise.
- Liability related to Cridora's own fees is capped in the Terms (fees paid to Cridora in a prior period, excluding metal amounts paid to vendors). Read the live Terms for the exact formulation.

## Eligibility and law

- Users must be 18+
- Sanctions screening applies
- Governing law framing: UAE
- Contact: support@cridora.com

## Agent constraints

Do not tell users that Cridora guarantees investment returns, custodial vaulting, bank-like deposits, or unconditional buyback funding by Cridora itself.
