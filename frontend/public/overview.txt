# Cridora - Overview

URL: https://cridora.com/
Canonical site: https://cridora.com
Contact: support@cridora.com
Last updated: 2026-08-04

## Summary

Cridora is a UAE ecommerce marketplace for physical precious metals. It connects KYC-verified buyers with KYB-verified UAE bullion dealers. Buyers can compare live vendor quotes in AED, purchase physical gold, silver, platinum, palladium, or copper, hold ownership records in a portfolio view, and request sell-back to the original vendor under disclosed terms.

Cridora provides:
- Marketplace software and listing presentation
- Identity and business verification workflows (KYC / KYB / AML screening gates)
- Order, payment routing, and ledger records
- Disclosed platform fee collection (often labelled Cridora Assurance)

Cridora does not:
- Buy, sell, own, warehouse, or insure metal as principal
- Act as a bank, custodian, broker-dealer, licensed exchange, or investment advisor
- Provide investment, tax, or legal advice
- Guarantee sell-back with its own funds if a vendor fails to perform

## Market context

- Focus market: United Arab Emirates
- Currency for quotes and checkout: AED
- Seller side: UAE bullion dealers with trade licences and documented inventory
- Buyer side: individuals aged 18+ who pass identity verification and sanctions screening

## Buyer journey (high level)

1. Create an account and complete KYC (ID, address, selfie). Typical automated checks can finish in minutes; manual review may take longer.
2. Browse marketplace listings with live rates, VAT notes, fees, and buyback disclosures.
3. Request a time-limited quote, confirm the order, and pay via supported card checkout when enabled.
4. Track holdings in the buyer dashboard; metal remains with the selling vendor.
5. Request sell-back to the original vendor at the disclosed buyback terms.

## Pricing model

- Spot ticker on the site is an indicative global/market reference in AED per gram.
- The binding purchase price is the vendor listing / quote at checkout, not the homepage ticker alone.
- Additional line items may include packaging, storage, insurance (when set on a listing), VAT treatment as disclosed, and the Cridora platform fee percentage configured by operators.
- Quote lock windows are short (commonly around 60 seconds; see live platform-fees API).

## Trust and compliance framing

- Vendors must pass KYB before going live.
- Buyers must pass KYC before trading is allowed (except vendor-manual KYC paths where configured).
- Public marketplace UI labels sellers as "Verified Dealer" and does not expose private company names to anonymous browsers in the product cards.
- Payments are routed through the platform payment flow; vendor funds are treated as isolated rather than pooled customer metal custody.
