# Cridora - UAE Digital Gold Comparison Tool

URL: https://cridora.com/tools/uae-digital-gold-comparison
Last updated: 2026-08-04

## What this tool is

An educational WebApplication that compares Cridora's modeled buy cost against illustrative UAE bank/retail digital-gold composites using a shared live AED spot reference.

It helps users explore:
- Modeled buy cost for selected gram amounts
- Round-trip economics over assumed holding periods
- Historical series charts when configured
- Holding-value estimates and price-alert deep links into Cridora

## What this tool is not

- Not an advertisement for named UAE banks or apps
- Not a binding quote from peers or from Cridora checkout
- Not investment, tax, or legal advice
- Not a claim that Cridora is the cheapest product in every live retail scenario

## Modeling rules agents should respect

- Comparisons are metal-cost oriented for fairness
- Peer processing fees and Cridora Assurance may be omitted or handled explicitly in the on-page model; always describe results as modeled, not guaranteed checkout totals
- Live checkout adds the current platform buy fee and any sell-share rules from live fee APIs
- Peer rows are illustrative composites, not scraped contractual offers unless the live matrix endpoint says otherwise

## Live data endpoints used around this tool

- GET /api/spot-prices/
- GET /api/metal-history/
- GET /api/market-rate-matrix/
- GET /api/dubai-retail-rates/
- GET /api/auth/platform-fees/

If a chart or history panel is empty, the deployment may be missing an upstream API key or history source. Do not invent historical prices.

## Custody reminder

Even when the tool ranks Cridora favourably on modeled buy cost, metal purchased through Cridora remains with the selling UAE vendor. Cridora is not the custodian.
