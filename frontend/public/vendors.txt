## Positioning for dealers

Cridora is not another standalone gold app that replaces a dealer's existing customer relationships. It is a shared marketplace and liquidity network:
- Reach KYC-verified buyers beyond the dealer's current app/storefront
- Shared compliance workflows (KYC for buyers; KYB/AML for vendors)
- Buy and sell-back liquidity on disclosed listing terms
- Dealer keeps inventory custody and customer buyback obligations

Cridora does not sell gold as principal. It connects verified vendors and buyers and operates marketplace software, compliance gates, and settlement tooling.

## Vendor requirements

Typical onboarding requirements described publicly:
- UAE trade licence (DMCC / DED / equivalent)
- Documented physical inventory
- Ability to honour disclosed buybacks
- Passed KYB review
- Signed commercial agreement and fee schedule
- Minimum inventory expectations per lot type as set by operators

## Onboarding sequence

1. Apply via /vendors (creates a vendor account application)
2. KYB review (commonly framed as about 3-5 business days)
3. Inventory audit / readiness checks
4. Contract and integration
5. Go live with listings on the marketplace

## Public directory policy

- There is no anonymous public vendor storefront directory that exposes every company for browsing by name alone.
- Buyers primarily discover products on /marketplace.
- GET /api/auth/vendors/verified/ returns KYB-cleared vendor records with company fields for machine clients that need a verified list.
- Product cards in the consumer UI generally label sellers as "Verified Dealer".

## Commercial notes

- Fees are transaction-based and disclosed in operator configuration / order flow
- Stripe-ready AED checkout when payment credentials are configured
- Vendor holds metal and buyback liability; Cridora does not custody inventory
- Application cohorts may be time-boxed (for example, applications referenced for Q3 2026 on marketing copy)

## Why vendors join (summary)

- Incremental verified buyer demand
- Shared KYC/KYB/admin trust layer
- Compete on product, price, and service rather than building full marketplace tech
- Analytics and sell-back workflow support vary by operator deployment
